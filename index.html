Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 31, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how NQJS ("we," "us," or "our") handles information in the games listed below.

Games covered by this policy

  • Garbage: Classic Card Game
  • Rocket Rugby: First Kicks
  • You Will Fail

Information we collect

The covered games do not require an account and do not collect or transmit personal information to us.

The games save gameplay information locally on your device, including settings, scores, game statistics, virtual coins, selected categories or topics, and unlocked content. This information is used only to provide game features and preserve your progress. It is not transmitted to us or shared with third parties.

Analytics, advertising, and purchases

The covered games do not currently use advertising, analytics, cloud diagnostics, in-app purchasing, or third-party account services.

Device permissions

The covered games do not request access to your precise location, contacts, camera, or microphone.

Data retention and deletion

Locally saved gameplay information remains on your device until you reset the game data, clear the app's storage, or uninstall the game. Because this information is not sent to us, we cannot access or delete it remotely.

Children's privacy

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through the covered games. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us outside a game, contact us so we can review and address the request.

Security

We limit data handling by keeping gameplay progress on the user's device and by not operating a server that receives gameplay data. No method of electronic storage is completely secure, but we take reasonable steps appropriate to the limited information handled by the games.

Platform services

The store or operating-system provider through which you download a game may process information independently under its own privacy policy. We do not control that provider's data practices.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy if a covered game or its data practices change. We will post the revised policy on this page and update the date above.
